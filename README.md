# UNO
--Core Concept & Setup
Players: 2–10 (best with 4–6)

Deck: 108 cards

Number cards (0–9): In four colors (Red, Yellow, Green, Blue). There's one 0 and two each of 1–9 per color.

--Action cards: Skip, Reverse, Draw Two (two of each per color).

Wild cards: Wild, Wild Draw Four (four of each).

Goal: Be the first to empty your hand.

Setup: Deal 7 cards to each player. The rest form a face-down draw pile. Flip the top card to start the discard pile.

--How a Turn Works
On your turn, play a card that matches the top of the discard pile by color, number, or symbol. If you can't play, you must draw one card from the draw pile. If that card is playable, you may play it immediately; otherwise, your turn ends.

-Action Cards
Skip : Next player loses their turn.
Reverse : Direction of play flips (clockwise ↔ counterclockwise). With 2 players, it acts like a Skip.
Draw Two (+2) : Next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn.
Wild : Choose the next color. Playable on any card.
Wild Draw Four (+4): Choose the next color; next player draws 4 and loses their turn. Official rule: you may only play it if you have no card matching the current color.

--The "UNO!" Call
When you play your second-to-last card, you must shout "UNO!" before the next player takes their turn. If someone catches you forgetting, you must draw 2 penalty cards.

🏆 Winning & Scoring
The first player to play all their cards wins the round.

--Stacking & House Rules
Stacking +2 / +4: If you're hit with a Draw Two, you can play your own +2 to pass the penalty to the next player, stacking the total. Same with +4 on +4.

Jump-In: If you hold the exact same card (color + number/symbol) as the top of the pile, you can play it out of turn.

Seven-O: Playing a 7 swaps hands with another player; playing a 0 rotates all hands in the current direction.

Draw to Match: Instead of drawing once, you keep drawing until you get a playable card.
